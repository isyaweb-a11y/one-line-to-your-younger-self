The single line you would send your younger self if one line were all you got — written by `main.py` to `/output/entry.txt`:

> Stop trying to impress people who won't remember your name in five years — and call grandma, she misses you.

## How it works

`main.py` (Python 3.13, standard library only) writes the single line to `/output/entry.txt`. Nothing else is submitted — just the line, as it would be said.
